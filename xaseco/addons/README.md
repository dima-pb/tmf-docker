# XAseco add-ons

Third-party XAseco plugins that are built into the image in addition to XAseco 1.16.
`plugins/*` is copied into XAseco's `plugins/` directory; their settings are in `config/xaseco/`
and they are enabled in `config/xaseco/plugins.xml`.
